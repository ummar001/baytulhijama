What is Acupuncture?

Acupuncture is a key part of Traditional Chinese Medicine that has been practised for over 2,000 years. Fine, sterile needles are inserted at specific points on the body to stimulate the flow of energy (Qi), release natural pain-relieving chemicals, and restore balance.

At Baytul Hijama we combine classical acupuncture techniques with a modern, evidence-informed approach to help with pain, stress, migraines, fertility support, and overall wellbeing.

Every treatment is tailored to you after a full consultation.
